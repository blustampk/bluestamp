Your document

What happens to the file you send

  • Who opens it: me, nobody else. One person, not a team, not a contractor.
  • Where it goes: my laptop in Dubai, and the document-reading service I build with (Anthropic), which runs outside the UAE, keeps its logs for up to 30 days and does not train on them.
  • What is never done with it: not shown to anyone, not used as an example, not put in a shared folder.
  • When it is deleted: seven days after your video, from my machine, whether or not you write back.

Redacted or dummy copies are welcome; the video will use whatever is left readable. Personal data in the document is handled under the UAE Personal Data Protection Law (Federal Decree-Law 45 of 2021). If a file arrives with an ID or passport number you missed, I black it out before it is read.

Questions you’d ask on a call

Since there isn’t one

What if it arrives as a photo or a scan?
Fine. Phone photos, scans, PDFs, emails. If part of it is unreadable, the video shows that too, and I’ll say what would help.
Which systems can it put things into?
Spreadsheets (Excel, Google Sheets), Zoho Books, and anything with an import, an export or an inbox: CSV files, email drafts, portal forms your staff paste into. If your system has no way in, I’ll say so before you pay anything.
What happens when a supplier changes their layout?
It flags what it couldn’t read instead of guessing. I fix it, usually the same day. That is what the monthly fee is for.
Who fixes it, and how fast?
Me. Same working day, Dubai time, Monday to Friday. There is no ticket queue because there is no one else.
What does the monthly fee cover, and how much is it?
Every document checked, fixes when a layout or a form changes, and me on WhatsApp during working hours. From AED 2,750 a month. The exact figure is in the one-page summary after the video, and it depends on how many outputs you want and whether they land in a system rather than a sheet.
Can I stop? What do I keep?
Any month, in writing. You keep the tool, its settings and every file it produced. Nothing is held hostage.
Do my staff need to install anything?
No. Documents go in the way they arrive now: a folder, an inbox, a WhatsApp forward. The result lands where you asked.
Can I see it on my own documents before paying?
That is the video. Nothing is built until you’ve seen it, and the setup fee is due only when the tool does on your real documents what the video showed.
